Changes: the IUoC app regenerated the classifier annotations against its exact
Tanzil Uthmani v1.1 text, mapping offsets back from the classification copy
onto the full, unchanged display text. The website exports those annotations
from the app database and splits them by chapter alongside the same text.

These are algorithmic annotations, not scholar-verified. Colouring is an
optional reading aid, not part of the Quran text. Colours and grouping are
shared with the IUoC app; preferences are stored in this browser only.
